01 / Research focus

From membrane
biology to disease.

Investigating a novel mechanism for extrahepatic inflammation in progressive familial intrahepatic cholestasis type 1 (PFIC1).

My doctoral research focuses on inflammatory disease associated with mutations in P4-ATPase, particularly ATP8B1. The goal is to understand the mechanisms behind inflammation and identify potential therapeutic targets.

Earlier research at Pokhara University examined vitamin D levels in patients with type 2 diabetes, using biochemical tests, ELISA, venipuncture, and SPSS analysis.

02 / Laboratory expertise

A practical, multidisciplinary toolkit.

Molecular & cellular biology

Cell culture, protein extraction, western blot, RNA and DNA extraction, plasmid isolation, gel electrophoresis, and qPCR.

Immunology & biochemical assays

Flow cytometry, ELISA, liposome reconstitution, and UV–Vis spectroscopy techniques including FRET.

Microscopy & image analysis

STEDYCON and confocal microscopy, immunofluorescence, lipid flip assays, and live cell imaging. Image analysis with ImageJ, Fiji, and NIS-Elements.

Animal research

Mouse breeding and genotyping, intraperitoneal and tail-vein injections, peritoneal lavage, blood and tissue harvesting, and cryosectioning.

Data analysis & scientific graphics

Origin, GraphPad Prism, SPSS, and Adobe Illustrator for analysis and visual communication.

Mentoring & laboratory operations

Undergraduate researcher training, laboratory maintenance, reagent ordering, stock management, and laboratory safety.
